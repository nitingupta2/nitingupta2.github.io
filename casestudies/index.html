Bank Marketing - Part 1 - Making the call - An Exploratory Data Analysis

Other posts in this series: Bank Marketing - Part 2 - Who will say yes? - Building Classification Models A Portuguese bank ran a series of telemarketing campaigns between May 2008 and November 2010. Its staff called clients and asked them to open a term deposit, a savings account that pays a fixed interest rate for a fixed period. The Bank Marketing dataset (Moro, Cortez and Rita, 2014) records 41,188 of these calls: who the client was, how and when they were called, the state of the economy at the time, and whether the client said yes.

The version of the dataset that we use (from Kaggle) does not have the location of each property.
